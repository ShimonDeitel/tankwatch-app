Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Tankwatch is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and, just as importantly, what it doesn't.

What Tankwatch stores

Every tank you add and every level-check reading you log is stored locally on your device. Tankwatch does not operate a server or cloud backend, and this data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Notifications

If you use up your free monthly logs, Tankwatch may ask permission to send you a single local reminder about upgrading to Pro. This notification is scheduled entirely on your device using Apple's notification framework — no data about you is sent anywhere to make this happen, and you can decline the permission request at any time.

Subscriptions and payment

Tankwatch Pro is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through the Apple App Store using Apple's StoreKit framework. All payment information is handled directly by Apple; Tankwatch never sees or stores your payment details. Purchase and subscription-status information is verified locally against Apple's records.

Analytics and advertising

Tankwatch does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data deletion

Because all data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your tank and reading history. There is no account to close and no server-side record to request deletion of.

Children's privacy

Tankwatch is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@tankwatchapp.com.
